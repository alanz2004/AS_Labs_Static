What stays. What goes. Honestly.

Anyone promising to replace your entire stack is selling you a broken calendar. The audit draws this line for your specific operation.

Stays — it earns its keep

  • Channel sync — real-time Airbnb/Booking/Vrbo rates & availability need certified API partners. Non-negotiable.
  • Dynamic pricing — if it's lifting RevPAR 10–20%, it's paying for itself. We audit it, we don't touch it.
  • Payment rails — OTA payouts and card processing stay where they are.

Goes — you own it instead

  • Guest messaging labor — the 40% of messages templates can't answer, drafted by agents from your unit data, approved by your team.
  • Owner statements — generated from reservations and expenses, in the owner's currency and language. Statement day becomes a review.
  • Maintenance intake — guest complaint → ticket → vendor dispatch → owner cost-approval, one chain.
  • Overpriced PMS tiers — often the audit's finding is "downgrade the plan, agent the gap."
