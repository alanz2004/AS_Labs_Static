Agent 02 · ancillary revenue

The Upsell Agent

Early check-in, late checkout, mid-stay cleaning, airport pickup, local tours — offered at the moment they're most likely to be wanted, not buried in a welcome email. The agent reads the stay context (arrival time, party size, length of stay) and times each offer.

Industry benchmark for AI-timed upsell offers: 15–20% conversion. This is pure margin on inventory you already own — an empty morning becomes a paid early check-in.

Repeat-Guest Reactivation

The first booking comes from Airbnb and pays Airbnb. The second one doesn't have to. The agent works your past-guest list — captured at every stay — and writes personal win-back messages with a returning-guest direct rate, timed to each guest's booking pattern (same season, anniversaries, holiday windows).

This is the most underused lever in the industry — most operators collect the emails and never use them. A management company with years of history is sitting on its cheapest acquisition channel.
