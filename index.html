Agent 01 · speed-to-booking

Direct-Booking Conversational Sales Agent

Most high-intent visitors browse your direct site but leave when they hit a quick question — elevator access, distance to Gordon Beach, whether it works for a family — or because they want a custom quote for a multi-week stay. By the time a human replies hours later, they've booked on Airbnb. This agent closes that gap in seconds, in the visitor's own language.

1

Instant multilingual answers

Engages visitors in English, French, Hebrew, German, or Russian — answering granular apartment questions from your live portfolio data, not canned FAQs.

2

Smart quote generator

Builds a custom, shareable quote page or WhatsApp payment link on the spot — tailored dates, bundled perks (free airport pickup, late checkout), and a direct-booking discount.

3

Abandoned-cart recovery

If a visitor gets a quote but doesn't check out, the agent follows up on WhatsApp or email at 2 hours and 24 hours with a targeted incentive.

4

Human handoff

Complex or non-standard requests escalate to your team instantly — the agent knows the edge of what it should answer.

Worked example · a Tuesday, 23:41

Visitor (French) → "Rothschild 33 est proche de la plage Gordon ? Ascenseur ? Famille de 4, 2 semaines en aout."
▸ agent detects French · pulls Rothschild 33: 12-min walk to Gordon, elevator (floor 3), sleeps 4, free crib
Agent (French) → Answers both questions, then offers a 14-night direct quote at a better-than-platform rate.
▸ visitor says yes → generates quote QT-4471 + Stripe link
📄 Quote: 14 nights · ₪560/night direct (vs ₪665 platform) · free airport pickup · guest saves ₪1,470 · link holds dates 48h
▸ if unpaid → auto WhatsApp at +2h ("still held, want a free late checkout?") → visitor returns & books
Result
Direct booking ₪7,840 · ~₪1,176 OTA commission avoided on this one stay

Agent 02 · revenue optimization

Dynamic Pricing & Revenue Agent

A flat nightly rate is a guess that's wrong twice: too low when a tech conference floods the city, too high when a random Tuesday sits empty. This agent reprices every open night against real demand signals — booking velocity, local events, competitor rates, occupancy gaps — inside the min/max guardrails you set, and explains every change in plain language so you're never guessing why a night moved.

1

Reads live demand

Tracks booking velocity, seasonality, and how fast comparable units nearby are filling — the signals a human can't watch continuously across a whole portfolio.

2

Catches events early

Detects Tel Aviv Tech Week, holidays, and demand surges before the search spike — raising rates while the window is open, not after everyone's booked.

3

Fills the gaps

Spots soft mid-week nights that would otherwise sit empty and nudges the rate down just enough to fill them — occupancy you'd have lost at a flat price.

4

Stays inside your rules

You set floors and ceilings and season strategy; the agent only moves within them, and shows its reasoning on every night. Long-term positioning stays yours.

5

Prices by occupancy, learns from history

An AI model watches how full each future month is filling and weighs it against your past prices and what those rates actually earned. If next month is already fully booked far out, that's the signal you left money on the table — so it raises rates on the remaining open dates and future months. If a month is filling slowly, it eases prices to protect occupancy. Every rate is chosen from occupancy plus price history, not a fixed rule.

Worked example · HaYarkon 156, next 14 nights

Flat rate today: ₪600/night — same price whether it's Tech Week or a dead Tuesday.
Sep 12-14 (Tech Week) → raised to ₪820-880 — comps at ₪720, velocity +40%. Nights that would've under-earned at ₪600.
Sep 9-10 (soft mid-week) → dropped to ₪540 — two empty nights filled instead of sitting vacant at ₪600.
▸ every other night nudged up or held, each with a one-line reason you can read
Result
15-30% more revenue from the same 14 nights — by charging more when demand is high and filling gaps when it's soft

Agent 03 · the second booking

Predictive LTV & Re-Booking Campaign Agent

Past guests are your cheapest high-margin revenue — yet most operators send generic email blasts that get ignored. This agent tracks stay history to run micro-targeted direct-booking campaigns that arrive exactly when each guest starts planning their next trip.

1

Behavioral segmentation

Groups past guests by origin country, stay frequency, group type (families vs couples), and season of visit — the axes that predict how and when they rebook.

2

Predictive timing

Learns each segment's travel cycle — European families book summer Tel Aviv trips ~120 days ahead — and fires outreach at the exact window they begin planning.

3

VIP loyalty offers

Returning guests get exclusive direct rates, free upgrades, or priority early check-in — bypassing third-party channels entirely on the highest-margin bookings you have.

4

Human touch where it counts

Your highest-LTV guests (3+ stays) get flagged for a personal note rather than automation — the agent drafts it and times it; you send it.
